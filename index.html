System Trustworthiness and AI Reliability

Building computing systems people can trust.

STAR Lab at George Mason University studies how to make AI models, the software they write, and the systems they run on reliable, secure, and correct.

Publications GitHub

Research

AI reliability

Robustness, correctness, and failure analysis of machine learning and LLM-based systems.

Software engineering

Testing, analysis, and repair of software, both for AI systems and using AI.

System security

Finding and preventing vulnerabilities in real-world software and infrastructure.

ML systems

Efficient and dependable infrastructure for training and serving models.
